How is WPM calculated?

WPM uses the standard formula: correctly typed characters ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. Accuracy is the percentage of typed characters that match the passage at the same position. Errors count mismatched characters, including extra characters.

For the best experience, use a physical keyboard. No text is uploaded to a server.
