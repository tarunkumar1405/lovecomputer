LAN Wire & RJ45 Connector Kaise Banaye?

T568B color code ke saath LAN cable banane, RJ45 connector lagane aur cable test karne ka simple practical guide.

ADVERTISEMENT

Agar aap ghar ya office ke liye Ethernet/LAN cable banana chahte hain, to RJ45 connector ko sahi color sequence mein crimp karna zaroori hai. Is guide mein T568B standard ke through poora process simple steps mein diya gaya hai.

Quick rule: Straight-through cable ke liye dono ends par same T568B order use karein.

1. Kya-Kya Chahiye?

  • CAT5e ya CAT6 Ethernet cable
  • RJ45 connectors
  • RJ45 crimping tool
  • Cable tester
  • Cable cutter/stripper

3. LAN Cable Banane Ke Steps

Step 1 — Cable Strip Karein

✂️

Outer jacket ko carefully 2–3 cm remove karein. Andar ke conductors ko cut na karein.

Step 2 — Wires Straight Karein

〰️

8 wires ko separate karke gently straight karein.

Step 3 — T568B Order

🟧🟩🟦🟫

Pin 1–8 ke according White-Orange se Brown tak order set karein.

Step 4 — Same Length Cut

✂️

Sab wires ke ends ko ek straight line mein cut karein.

Step 5 — RJ45 Mein Insert

🔌

Color order check karke wires ko connector mein poori tarah insert karein.

Step 6 — Crimp

🛠️

Connector ko crimping tool mein rakhkar firmly press karein.

5. Cable Tester Se Check Karein

Crimping ke baad dono ends ko cable tester mein lagayein. Tester par pins 1 se 8 correct sequence mein show honi chahiye.

Agar sequence galat hai: connector ka color order check karein. Agar wire connector mein properly nahi baithi hai, connector ko dobara crimp karein.

6. Straight-Through vs Crossover

TypeEnd 1End 2
Straight-throughT568BT568B
CrossoverT568AT568B

Modern network equipment mein Auto-MDI/MDI-X support common hai, isliye normal PC-to-router/switch connection ke liye straight-through cable generally practical choice hai.

8. FAQ

RJ45 mein kitne pins hote hain?

Ethernet RJ45 connector mein 8 contact positions hoti hain.

T568B order kya hai?

White-Orange, Orange, White-Green, Blue, White-Blue, Green, White-Brown, Brown.

CAT5e aur CAT6 dono use kar sakte hain?

Haan, compatible RJ45 connector aur cable category ka dhyan rakhein.

Kya cable tester zaroori hai?

Mandatory nahi, lekin crimping error identify karne ke liye strongly useful hai.

Conclusion

RJ45 connector lagate waqt sabse important cheez correct T568B sequence hai. Wires ko properly arrange, insert aur crimp karne ke baad cable tester se verify karna best practice hai.
