Microphone Awaaz Ko Digital Data Mein Kaise Badalta Hai?

Microphone sound pressure ko electrical signal aur ADC digital samples mein kaise convert karta hai.

Microphone sound pressure ko electrical signal aur ADC digital samples mein kaise convert karta hai.

Simple flow: User action → Device hardware → Controller/processing → Data transfer or output

Sound kya hai?

Sound air pressure variations ke roop mein travel karta hai. Microphone diaphragm in pressure changes ko mechanical movement mein convert karta hai.

Microphone transducer

Microphone type ke according diaphragm movement electrical signal mein convert hota hai. Ye initially analog signal ho sakta hai.

ADC ka role

Analog-to-digital converter signal ko regular time intervals par sample karta hai aur each sample ko numeric value mein quantize karta hai.

Sample rate aur bit depth

Sample rate batata hai kitne samples per second liye jate hain. Bit depth each sample ki amplitude representation ki precision affect karti hai.

Digital audio

Digital samples ko processing, storage aur transmission ke liye encoded audio stream mein use kiya ja sakta hai.

FAQ

Is topic ko samajhna kyun useful hai?

Is device ka basic working samajhne se common performance, connectivity aur storage behavior ko samajhna easier hota hai.

Kya actual performance specifications par depend karti hai?

Haan. Hardware generation, interface, controller, workload aur connected system ke capabilities ke according real-world performance change ho sakti hai.
