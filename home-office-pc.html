COMPUTER CONFIGURATION

Home & Office PC

Balanced configuration for browsing, documents, meetings, accounting, study and everyday work.

Recommended Configuration

CPUModern 4–6 core processor
RAM16 GB recommended
Storage512 GB NVMe SSD or larger
GPUIntegrated graphics is sufficient for normal office work
Display23–24 inch Full HD IPS monitor
PSUQuality 450–550 W unit for a desktop

💡 What to Prioritize

Do not choose components only by model name. Match the processor, RAM, storage, graphics, power supply and cooling to the actual workload.

🔧 Upgrade Planning

Before buying a desktop, check motherboard RAM slots, M.2 slots, PSU capacity, case clearance and cooling support so future upgrades remain possible.

💾 Storage & Backup

An SSD improves everyday responsiveness. Important documents, projects and datasets should also have a separate backup.

⚠️ Before Buying

Prices and component availability change. Treat this page as a specification guide and compare current models and prices before purchasing.
